Vaclav works on human motion and pose transfer, studying how synthetic video can support action understanding and accessibility. He has shown that synthetic human-action data improves classification through pose transfer [[synthetic-human-action]], examined how realistic pose-transfer-generated motion actually is [[pose-transfer-realism]], and built a pose-aware language-model interface that gives feedback to sign language learners [[pose-aware-llm-asl]].
