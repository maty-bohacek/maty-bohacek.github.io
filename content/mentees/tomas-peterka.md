Tomas worked on media provenance, using metadata and language models to assess whether images and videos are relevant to the news stories they accompany. He built a dataset of news articles with provenance metadata [[news-provenance-dataset]] and developed LLM-based methods for determining media relevance from provenance signals [[llm-provenance-metadata]].
